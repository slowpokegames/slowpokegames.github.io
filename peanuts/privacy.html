Peanuts: A Sliding Puzzle

Privacy policy · Slowpoke Games · November 2026

What the game collects

Nothing. Peanuts has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, send or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, the Looks you have won and your achievements are saved on your own device so the game can remember them. They never leave it, except:

  • when you choose to export your progress to a file, which goes only where you save or share it;
  • on Android, if you have backups turned on, as part of your device's own backup to your Google account;
  • on Steam, through Steam Cloud and Steam achievements, which Valve's privacy policy covers.

Uninstalling the game deletes its save from your device.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first.
